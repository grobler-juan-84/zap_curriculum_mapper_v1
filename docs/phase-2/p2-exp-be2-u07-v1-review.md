## 1. Executive unit interpretation

**Teacher-readable summary (`AI_INTERPRETATION`, grounded below):**

Unit 7 teaches children to **talk about food likes and dislikes** (*Do/Does … like …?*), to **use *a / an / some*** with **countable and uncountable** food nouns, and to **offer, accept, request, or politely decline** food (*Do you want …?*, *Yes, please / No, thanks*, *I want …, please*, *No … for me, thanks*). Supporting strands: **healthy vs unhealthy snacks** (CLIL), **where fruits come from** (culture), **choose healthy foods** (values), and **ee / ie** phonics.

Unlike Unit 6 (clocks + timed routines), Unit 7’s communicative core is **preference + quantity + food offers**, not time scheduling.

### 6.2 Plausible Korean EFL — `AI_PREDICTED_DIFFICULTY` (hypotheses only)

| Hypothesis | Linguistic reason | LRs |
|---|---|---|
| Auxiliary *do/does* in preference questions feels unnatural | Korean yes/no preference often without English-style auxiliaries | LR-02, LR-04 |
| Countability is opaque (*bread/meat/cheese* uncountable vs Korean classifier habits) | Noun count status is language-specific; English *some* patterns differ | LR-06 |
| *a/an* before vowel sounds | Phonological article rule not mirrored in Korean | LR-06 |
| *ee* vs *ie* spelling for /iː/ | Multiple English spellings for similar vowels | LR-14 |

## 9. Teacher-facing synthesis

**Unit 7 in one glance (AI self-assessment — not teacher-validated):**

1. **Food words** — fruits, vegetables, snacks, category words.  
2. **Likes:** *Do you like …?* / *Does he/she like …?* with short answers.  
3. **Watch:** *do/does*, *like/likes*, *doesn’t like*.  
4. **Count vs uncount** with ***a / an / some***.  
5. **Offers:** *Do you want a/an/some …?* → *Yes, please / No, thanks*.  
6. **Healthy choices:** *I want …, please* / *No … for me, thanks*.  
7. **Also teach (don’t skip):** healthy/unhealthy snacks CLIL, fruit origins, values, *ee/ie* phonics.  
8. **Likely stickies:** countability + articles; mixing *like* vs *want*.

## 12. Lessons learned and recommendations for Unit 8

1. Start each unit with a **language-systems inventory** (U6: time/routines/Wh; U7: likes/countability/offers) before LR drafting.  
2. Never target a fixed LR count.  
3. Keep R-C exposure default for text-only lemmas.  
4. Preserve G1–G4 comparison when testing a third unit (U8 *Wild Animals* — modality `can/can’t`).  
5. For U8, expect adaptation around **ability modals** and **adjective order**; do not force food-offer or clock frames.  
6. Still do **not** lock a Phase 2 schema after two units; wait for U8 + owner review.  
7. Optional later: teacher validation pass on §9-style syntheses for U6+U7.
