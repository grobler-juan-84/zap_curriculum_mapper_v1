## 2. Executive unit interpretation

**Teacher-readable summary (`AI_INTERPRETATION`):**

Unit 1 introduces **classroom object vocabulary**, teaches students to **identify singular and plural objects** (*What is it? / What are they?*) often with **colors**, to **talk about possessions** (*I have …*, *How many … do you have?*), to use **classroom imperatives** (*Sit down! / Don’t run!*), and to use **polite classroom language** (*Please …*, *Thank you.*, *You’re welcome.*). Supporting strands: school possessions CLIL/math counting, classrooms around the world (culture), values *Be polite.*, and phonics *a, t, p, n*.
