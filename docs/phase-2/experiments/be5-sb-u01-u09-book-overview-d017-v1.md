## B. Whole-book snapshot

Big English 5 SB keeps the Big English instructional spine (vocabulary → reading → Language in Action / Grammar → CLIL → Culture → Life Skills → phonics → Review / I Can), with denser themes around interests, family life events, fundraising, shopping, vacations, future technology, gadgets, product origins, and adventure.

Grammar expands into **gerunds after verbs/prepositions**, **tense review**, **used to**, **could / be going to**, **present perfect**, **comparatives/superlatives**, **past continuous**, **will** predictions, **may/might** and **used for/to**, **zero conditional**, **passive**, **relative clauses**, and **present perfect with for/since**.

This overview summarizes **what the book teaches unit by unit**. It does **not** claim mastery of earlier Big English levels.
