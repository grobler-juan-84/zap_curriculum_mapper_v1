## 2. Strengths and weaknesses

| Approach | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Restores writing as student outcomes; keeps BE2-like eight columns | Writing not scannable as a strand; denser LOs |
| **B** | Instant Writing visibility; short LOs; closest to publisher S&S layout feel | Writing absent from LOs; weaker “what students can do” completeness |
