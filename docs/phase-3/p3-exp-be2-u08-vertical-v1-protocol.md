## 2. Question

> What meaningful Previous → Current → Future connections can be stated for BE2 Unit 8 *Wild Animals* from Big English textbook evidence, focused on grammar, sentence structures, and communicative development — without forcing alignment?

---

## 3. Alignment philosophy (owner-agreed)

1. Grammar, sentence structures, and communicative development are primary.  
2. Familiar vocabulary may support new structures.  
3. Never force connections or require perfect alignment.  
4. When a useful connection appears, note it as a **potential teaching opportunity**.  
5. Different tenses/functions do not automatically invalidate a connection — but do **not** misrepresent them as direct grammatical progression.  
6. Prior textbook exposure does **not** establish student mastery.  
7. If no meaningful connection exists, **leave it unmapped**.
