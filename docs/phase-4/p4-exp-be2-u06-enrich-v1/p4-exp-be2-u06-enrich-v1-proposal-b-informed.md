## Opportunity B1 — Keep STR-01 (clock) and STR-02 (routines) separate

**What is being enriched or supported?**  
Same as A1: prevent blending clock questions with routine-time questions. Phase 2 names these **STR-01** vs **STR-02**.

**Why might students need it?**  
Same consideration as A1. V3.1 makes the separation the organizing spine of the unit structures—not a claim of student failure.

**Evidence:**  
- **Textbook:** `bep2_lang_u6_001` vs `bep2_lang_u6_002`/`003`.  
- **AI interpretation:** Distinct structure IDs and patterns in V3.1.  
- **Teacher judgment:** One contrast moment before focus practice.

**Smallest practical teacher action:**  
Same as A1: 30–60 seconds contrasting one STR-01 and one STR-02 example; today’s goal = STR-02.

**Priority class:** Optional reinforcement (conditional — if students mix the two).

**Unnecessary if…**  
Students already stay in STR-02 during Act 9–12 pair work.

## Opportunity B2 — Pair FS-02 (*do/does*) with FS-01 (*-s*) in one short cycle

**What is being enriched or supported?**  
Same as A2: full exchange accuracy. Phase 2 tags both as **explicit** foundational skills and frequent-reinforcement candidates.

**Why might students need it?**  
Same as A2: Act 10 isolates helper choice; answers need *-s*. V3.1’s “frequent reinforcement” flag is interpretive—not classroom evidence.

**Evidence:**  
- **Textbook:** Act 10; models with *-s*.  
- **AI interpretation:** FS-02 + FS-01; both `frequent_reinforcement_candidate: true`.  
- **Cross-unit (CON-02):** *-s* also appears later in U8 habitat facts (*lives* / *live*). Useful as **awareness**, not as a reason to teach U8 now or claim transfer.  
- **Teacher judgment:** Bind Q and A in one drill; optionally one forward sentence.

**Smallest practical teacher action:**  
Same 2–3 minute Q→A cycle as A2. **Optional add-on (CON-02 only):** one sentence—“You’ll see this same *-s* idea again later with animal facts”—no U8 materials.

**Priority class:** Optional reinforcement (conditional). CON-02 add-on = optional awareness (not a separate opportunity; not essential).

**Unnecessary if…**  
Full exchanges are already accurate in Act 12-style work. Skip the CON-02 sentence unless a quick forward link helps motivation/clarity.

**Declined from CON-01:** No Unit 1 Wh- review enrichment. CON-01’s shared “question word + helper before subject” habit is already inside today’s *When do/does…?* teaching; a separate U1 recycle would add load without a new focus decision.

---

## Opportunity B3 — Make FS-03 (*at*) audible because it is implicit in the book

**What is being enriched or supported?**  
Same as A3: *at* before clock times. Phase 2 labels FS-03 teaching status **implicit** and a frequent-reinforcement candidate.

**Why might students need it?**  
Same consideration as A3. The “implicit” label strengthens the *rationale* (easy to under-teach because there is no titled grammar box)—it does **not**, by itself, prove students omit *at* or make enrichment essential.

**Evidence:**  
- **Textbook:** *at* in every STR-02/STR-03 model answer; no Act-10-style *at* choice item.  
- **AI interpretation:** FS-03 `teaching_status: implicit`.  
- **Teacher judgment:** Stress *at* once when modelling; recast bare times.

**Smallest practical teacher action:**  
Same as A3: stress *at* on one model; recast once if bare times appear.

**Priority class:** Optional reinforcement (conditional — if bare times appear). Still **not** essential: implicit ≠ missing curriculum, and there is no student-performance evidence.

**Unnecessary if…**  
Students already produce *at + time* consistently.
