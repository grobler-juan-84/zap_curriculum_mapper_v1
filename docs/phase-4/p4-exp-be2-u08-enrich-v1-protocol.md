## 3. Method

1. Restate what the book already teaches (curriculum floor).  
2. Note predicted difficulties and useful vertical notes.  
3. Propose **at most 3–5** enrichment opportunities — fewer is fine; blank is valid.  
4. Tag: `BOOK_EVIDENCE` | `AI_INTERPRETATION` | `AI_PREDICTED_DIFFICULTY` | `MISSING_EVIDENCE`.  
5. Enrichment ≠ harder content. No forced vertical links. No duplicate activities without benefit.
