## 2. Experiment 11 — detailed handoff; no Create outline

**Path:** Paste detailed handoff on Topic. **Do not** click `Create outline`. Refine with fixed settings + five vocabulary chips. Generate.

### Findings

- Chalkie used its own internal lesson planning (no teacher outline supplied).
- Strong `Did…?` grammar focus; correct use of the five supplied phrases.
- Teach-first and book-consolidation-later is a **legitimate structure**, not a sequencing failure.
- Mostly source-safe and pedagogically useful.
- **Light teacher-review issue:** a statement that students should say `have`, “not eat or had,” is misleading — `Did you eat breakfast?` is valid; only `had` is incorrect after `Did`.

### Verdict

**PASS AFTER LIGHT TEACHER REVIEW**

---

## 3. Experiment 12 — Create outline clicked; Chalkie outline replaced with ours

**Path:** Same intended handoff/settings as Exp 11, but select `Create outline`, then replace Chalkie’s outline body with the local concise teacher-controlled outline.

### Findings

- After outline replacement, the **detailed handoff was no longer visible** in the main input field; the concise outline became the dominant/replacement input.
- Broader, freer, and more conversational than Experiment 11.
- Pedagogically valuable and usable as a teacher starting point — **not** a terrible lesson.
- Weaker provenance control: invented Luke/Amy listening dialogue; invented examples presented as Activities 10 and 12.
- Significant extension into general healthy/unhealthy habits.
- Treat as an **outline-replacement experiment**, **not** a valid “detailed handoff plus outline” test.

### Verdict

**PEDAGOGICALLY USABLE AFTER TEACHER REVIEW, but not the preferred source-controlled workflow**

## 4. Experiment 13 — detailed handoff with concise outline appended in the same initial input

**Path (follow-on after Exp 11–12):** Paste the detailed prompt **and** the concise teacher-controlled outline together into the **initial** Topic prompt field. **Do not** click `Create outline`. Same five vocabulary chips on Refine. Generate.

### Findings

- Best balance of grammar preparation, activity sequencing, official book/audio use, controlled practice, and final pair speaking.
- Begins book integration at a sensible point after activation and vocabulary.
- Strong qualitative success relative to Exp 11–12 for source-controlled Single Lesson use.

### Retained teacher-review items

1. PDF page 8 contains unrelated AI-generated speech-bubble text about a science project.
2. Pages 13–14 mention five questions but show only four phrase combinations and three completed questions.
3. Page 23 contains an unrelated “What is your favorite subject?” visual.
4. Page 27 uses supplementary invented examples under an Activity 12 heading; these must not be mistaken for verified publisher exercise items.

### Verdict

**PASS AFTER LIGHT TEACHER REVIEW — strong qualitative success**

## 5. Provisional preferred Chalkie Single Lesson workflow

Operational preference from Experiments 11–13 only. **Not** a permanent methodology lock. Teacher remains in the loop. Preserve **Beauty in Simplicity**.

1. Copy or enter the lesson title.
2. Paste the complete detailed handoff into Chalkie’s initial prompt box.
3. Append the concise teacher-controlled outline underneath it in the **same** box.
4. Do **not** click Chalkie’s `Create outline` option.
5. On Refine, manually enter the locally prepared vocabulary chips.
6. Keep unwanted suggested vocabulary and optional resources unselected.
7. Generate the lesson.
8. Conduct teacher review of:
   - grammar and answers;
   - answer reveals;
   - textbook/activity attribution;
   - invented publisher-like content;
   - text embedded inside AI-generated images;
   - lesson timing and where book work should occur.

### Practical comparison (this target only)

| Path | Role after review |
| --- | --- |
| Exp 11 (detailed only; no outline click) | Usable baseline; internal planning OK |
| Exp 12 (Create outline → replace with ours) | Usable pedagogically; weaker source control; **not preferred** |
| Exp 13 (detailed + outline appended; no outline click) | **Provisional preferred** for source-controlled Single Lesson |
