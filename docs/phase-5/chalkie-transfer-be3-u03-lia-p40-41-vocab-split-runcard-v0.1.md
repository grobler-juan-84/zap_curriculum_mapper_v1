### Primary controlled finding

The revised vocabulary allocation produced the intended lesson separation:

- Lesson 1 did **not** introduce `always / usually / sometimes / never` as new teaching.
- Lesson 2 did **not** reteach the complete eight-item chore vocabulary set.
- Lesson 2 still retrieved and used enough chore language to teach frequency adverbs coherently.
- The SB p.40 → p.41 instructional progression remained intact.

**Supported working finding:**

> Chalkie’s lesson-specific vocabulary chips materially influence what it treats as teachable content in each lesson. Per-lesson vocabulary allocation should therefore remain part of the provisional handoff pattern.

**Qualification:** one stochastic generation is not absolute causal proof, but this controlled follow-up provides **convincing practical evidence** when compared with Experiment 8.
