## 3. Concise teacher-controlled outlines

**Not slide-by-slide scripts.** Sequence + book integration only. Put **protected textbook work early** so definitions/matching/quizzes/reveals cannot consume the lesson.

### Lesson 1 — SB p.136 (Acts 19–20)

**Goal:** Students activate past-action meaning, complete Act 19 with official Track 159 (*came* only as required), practise the four representative regular-past spelling patterns, and finish ready to use those patterns.

1. **Activation (short):** Picture-led “what happened yesterday?” — light; no full irregular list.
2. **Model (brief):** Show the four patterns *start–started, cry–cried, like–liked, stop–stopped*. Label non-book examples `[SUPPLEMENTARY]`.
3. **Guided practice (limited):** One short oral/written turn per pattern only — then stop.
4. **Protected textbook time (EARLY):** SB **p.136** — Act **19** (official **Track 159**; teacher plays audio; no invented script) + Act **20** (students use the book chart/word box; do not recreate the chart as publisher slides).
5. **Purposeful communication (light):** Two–three student sentences using a regular past form from the patterns.
6. **Final check (short):** One accurate regular-past sentence; confirm readiness for p.137.

### Lesson 2 — SB p.137 (Acts 21–23)

**Goal:** Students consolidate regular past in Act 21, learn the verified irregular pairs, and produce a short yesterday exchange using both.

1. **Retrieval (short):** Quick recycle of Lesson 1 regular-past patterns (oral only — do not re-enter the L1 chip list).
2. **Model (brief):** Preview irregular pairs *go–went … put–put*; keep `[SB]` vs `[SUPPLEMENTARY]` clear.
3. **Guided practice (limited):** Brief base↔past recognition only — then open the book.
4. **Protected textbook time (EARLY):** SB **p.137** — Acts **21** (prefer *traveled*; accept *travelled* if the book does — no spelling mini-lesson), **22**, **23**.
5. **Purposeful communication:** Pair share — *Yesterday, I…* using at least one regular and one irregular past where natural.
6. **Final check (short):** One meaningful past-action exchange (not a quiz dump).
