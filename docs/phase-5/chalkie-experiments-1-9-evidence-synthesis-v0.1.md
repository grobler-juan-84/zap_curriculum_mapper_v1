## 3. Supported findings

### Strongly supported practical findings

- Chalkie retains or prioritizes information from the **initial topic/prompt stage**; replacing the later outline does **not** reliably erase conflicting initial context (Exp 1–2).
- **Title alignment alone** is insufficient when the initial prompt conflicts with the intended lesson (Exp 2).
- A **detailed, source-grounded initial handoff** plus a **concise teacher-controlled outline** has repeatedly produced usable results (Exp 5–6; transfer Exp 7–9).
- A **25-slide slide-by-slide script is not required** as the default outline; concise outlines can achieve comparable topic/sequence fidelity with less authoring (Exp 3 vs 5).
- Concise outlines should control **instructional sequence** and **protected textbook work** without scripting every slide (Exp 5–6).
- **Explicit vocabulary selection** materially influences generated lesson content (Exp 1–2 vocab visibility; Exp 8–9 allocation effect).
- Vocabulary should be allocated **per lesson**, not automatically duplicated across a series without instructional reason (Exp 8 confound → Exp 9 control).
- Lesson 2 may **retrieve** prior vocabulary without receiving the entire Lesson 1 list again (Exp 9).
- Reveal/animation states can **inflate PDF page counts**; totals above 25 are **not** automatic failures (recorded across Exp 1–9).
- Chalkie still tends to add definitions, matching, quizzes, answer reveals, and extra explanatory material (Exp 3–9 curation notes).
- Generated material must be marked `[SUPPLEMENTARY]`; `[SB]` must be reserved for **directly verified** Student Book evidence (Exp 9 provenance failures).
- Official audio must be **referenced**, not recreated, transcribed, or given invented answer keys (Exp 7–9 Track handling).
- Frequency adverbs must **not** be converted into rigid percentages or days-per-week scales unless the source explicitly provides them (Exp 9).
- Protected textbook work should appear **early enough** that generated preliminaries cannot displace it (Exp 9 L1 late book work ~PDF p.22).

### Hypotheses (not yet strongly established)

- Exact internal channel that retains conflicting context after outline edit (hidden prompt vs titles vs other UI state).
- Classroom superiority of any outline format.
- Platform determinism across replications beyond Exp 6’s one successful same-input repeat.
- Transfer to other publishers, grades, or non-Chalkie backends (Canva + ChatGPT, etc.).

## 4. Rejected or weakened approaches

Evidence **advises against** (practical guidance — not universal causal laws):

- Deliberately conflicting initial topic prompts when the intended lesson differs.
- Assuming a replacement outline fully overrides the initial prompt.
- Relying on the title alone to correct conflicting context.
- Accepting Chalkie’s auto-outline without teacher review when sequence/textbook integration matter (Exp 4 usable for topic, weaker for sequence control).
- Using a long slide-by-slide script as the **default** outline format.
- Giving every lesson the same combined vocabulary list without an instructional reason.
- Treating generated definitions, charts, examples, or answers as publisher content.
- Treating exported PDF page count as equivalent to requested slide count.

## 5. Current provisional workflow

Operational pattern only — **not** a locked methodology:

1. Verify target identity, pages, activities, language, vocabulary, and official audio from the evidence layer.
2. Prepare one detailed, source-grounded initial handoff.
3. Keep title, initial prompt, lesson allocation, and outlines **semantically aligned**.
4. Use one **concise** teacher-controlled outline per lesson — not a slide-by-slide script.
5. Allocate vocabulary **separately** for each lesson.
6. Mark `[SS]`, `[SB]`, and `[SUPPLEMENTARY]` distinctly.
7. Configure fixed Chalkie settings (Lesson Series defaults as used in Exp 5–9).
8. Review the auto-outline; replace with approved concise outlines where required.
9. Generate **once** after owner authorization.
10. Export and review: scope, sequence, book integration, provenance, added material, slide economy, teacher curation.
11. Record filenames and both **requested slide count** and **exported PDF page count**.
12. Do **not** treat generation as classroom validation.

## 6. Remaining uncertainties

- Stochastic variation between otherwise similar Chalkie runs.
- Consistency of transfer to other books, grades, lesson types, and publishers.
- Whether provenance labels survive generation consistently.
- Whether protected textbook time is practically sufficient in a live ≈40-minute lesson.
- How much post-generation teacher editing is normally required.
- Whether the workflow transfers effectively to Canva + ChatGPT or other future output platforms.
- What the eventual UI should expose as platform-specific versus platform-neutral fields.
