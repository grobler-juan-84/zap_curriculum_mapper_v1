## Purpose

Investigate whether Chalkie's **Single Lesson format and predetermined slide allocation** constrain instructional scaffolding even when the curriculum evidence and Book Intelligence input are adequate.

This is a working hypothesis, not a proven finding. A longer output does not automatically mean better instruction. The next experiments must examine how instructional space is used, distinguishing planned slides from exported PDF pages created by answer/reveal states.

This document changes the experimental roadmap only. It does **not** authorize lesson generation, change the curriculum architecture, invalidate V1–V3, alter the D018 rubric, or update `4-decisions.md`.

## Observed limitation and working hypothesis

The controlled Single Lesson experiments used 25 requested slides with Chalkie Default, Simple theme, Grade 3, English ability Grade 2, and Audio OFF. Their exported PDFs contained about 30–31 pages, including answer/reveal pages.

Substantial output space was used for textbook vocabulary introduction, Student Book and Workbook directions, answer/reveal states, transitions, and review. This may leave too little space for:

- staged language scaffolding;
- meaningful repetition and retrieval;
- guided and partner practice;
- independent production;
- differentiated enrichment.

**Working hypothesis:** some lesson-quality limits may arise from Chalkie's generation format and slide allocation, not only from the quality of the supplied curriculum intelligence.

The hypothesis remains unproven. Evaluation must inspect actual slide purpose and classroom pacing rather than infer quality from length alone.

## Experiment 1 — two substantial lessons

**Status:** **DESIGN DIRECTION APPROVED — GENERATION NOT AUTHORIZED**

| Setting | Proposed value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Chalkie | Confirmed |
| Generation type | Lesson Series | Direction approved |

| Per-lesson slide behavior | Same requested count applied per lesson | User-confirmed capability; do not assume different counts can be assigned within one series |

### Lesson 1 — Student Book: Introduce

- Introduce and develop recognition of all 12 target animals.
- Use picture-led vocabulary presentation and retrieval.
- Model the exact required **like + can** speaking frame.
- Move from repetition to guided speaking.
- Support the relevant SB pp. 128–129 activities.
- Avoid later Unit 8 content.

### Lesson 2 — Workbook: Practise

- Retrieve and recycle Lesson 1 vocabulary.
- Reinforce the same speaking frame.
- Move from guided practice toward more independent speaking.
- Support WB pp. 104–105.
- Include meaningful checking and consolidation.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition of Lesson 1 introduction slides.

The owner confirms that this **SB introduction → WB practice** structure matches the school's current teaching approach. Slides support the textbooks; they do not replace or reproduce them unnecessarily.

### Change

- Single Lesson → Lesson Series.
- One lesson → two lessons.
- Requested slide allocation.
- Explicit SB-introduction / WB-practice division.

Because several generation parameters change together, Experiment 1 is a **practical configuration comparison**, not a strict single-variable causal test. Any improvement must not be attributed solely to Lesson Series mode.

Historical V2b and V3 outputs remain comparison points.

## Evaluation design proposal

Preserve the original seven D018 criteria and historical scores. Add a separate instructional-quality section for Lesson Series experiments:

1. **Scaffolding quality** — progression from introduction/modeling through guided practice to independent production.
2. **Instructional slide allocation** — teaching/practice slides versus directions, transitions, repetition, and answer/reveal pages.
3. **Lesson coherence** — each lesson has a clear instructional purpose.
4. **SB/WB progression** — Lesson 2 meaningfully builds on Lesson 1.
5. **Student language production** — meaningful opportunities to speak and use the target language.
6. **Teacher usability** — practical real-classroom delivery.
7. **Platform limitations** — weaknesses classified as prompt-related, intelligence-related, format-related, or unresolved.

**Thresholds are not finalized.** Owner approval is required before scoring rules are locked for the experiment.

For every run, record both the requested Chalkie slide count and exported PDF page count. Identify answer/reveal states before judging length or instructional allocation.
