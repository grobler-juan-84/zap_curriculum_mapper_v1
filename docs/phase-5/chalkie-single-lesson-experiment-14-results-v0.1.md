## 2. Findings

- Successfully pre-teaches the vocabulary needed to read and understand the passage.
- Provides a useful conceptual introduction to calories and energy without reproducing the complete publisher passage.
- Directs students to the real Student Book and official audio.
- Supports Activities 13–18 in a sensible order.
- Includes prediction, vocabulary preparation, reading, notebook work, sorting, pair speaking, chart work, and review.
- Demonstrates that the provisional preferred workflow **transfers beyond grammar** (LiA) lessons.
- Generated material functions as **preparation and support**, not a textbook replacement.

### Light teacher-review notes

1. The second goal says, “Identify good and bad activities that keep you healthy and in shape.” This is ambiguous — it can sound as if bad activities also keep students healthy.
2. The calories-in explanation refers broadly to “healthy drinks”; water is healthy but contains no calories.
3. The statement that putting on weight causes someone to “feel tired” is too definite and should be softened.
4. Some calorie explanations are appropriately simplified for Grade 3 and should **not** automatically be treated as failures.

## 3. Verdict

**PASS AFTER LIGHT TEACHER REVIEW — strong non-grammar transfer success**

## 4. Ask Chalkie (optional post-generation review)

**Observed (owner screenshot):** after generation, the teacher challenged the ambiguous second goal by asking “Is this the right phrasing?” Chalkie’s **Ask Chalkie** feature correctly explained the ambiguity and suggested clearer goals along the lines of:

1. Explain what a calorie measures.
2. Sort activities into those that are good or bad for your body.

### Intended workflow position

1. The local system constructs the evidence-based handoff **without AI**.
2. Chalkie generates the lesson.
3. The teacher reviews the lesson.
4. When useful, the teacher may use **Ask Chalkie** to question or revise a **specific** slide.
5. The teacher remains responsible for approving the final wording.

### Boundaries

- **Optional**, not required — not a mandatory workflow gate.
- Post-generation review aid only — **not** part of constructing the handoff.
- Does **not** guarantee correctness.
- Teacher-in-the-loop and **Beauty in Simplicity** remain in force.
