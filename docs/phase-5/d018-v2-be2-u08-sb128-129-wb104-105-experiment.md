### Finding B — Audio provenance: significant concern

PDF pages **22–23** introduce a generated listening activity titled **“Zoo Friends”** with:

- Characters Mia and Ben.
- Three true/false statements.
- Generated answer explanations.
- An audio playback element.

Slides are labeled **`[SB]`**, although these characters, statements, and explanations were **not** established by the supplied SB p.129 evidence.

Do **not** claim the audio was verified or that the publisher supplied these items. Any Chalkie audio remains supplementary only — never official publisher audio.

### Finding C — Workbook contribution: promising but not yet judged

V2 output contains WB p.104 naming/matching preparation and book bridge; WB p.105 song-writing preparation and book bridge; and a short open-response writing activity.

A generated **definition-matching** exercise appears **supplementary** and must **not** be represented as an exact reproduction of the Workbook exercise.

Whether these additions genuinely improve book readiness vs V1 is deferred to **Step 188** (key V2 comparison question).

### Finding D — Lesson efficiency: possible concern

The lesson includes multiple animal vocabulary slides; a vocabulary recognition quiz with answer reveal; a separate matching activity with answer reveal; several sentence-frame practice slides; and a supplementary listening activity.

Visuals are generally appropriate, but the output may be longer than necessary for a short textbook-support lesson. Requires formal evaluation — **not** an automatic failure.

### Finding E — Scope

Output largely concentrates on the target animals, *chase*, animal abilities, the SB sentence frame, and WB practice.

No clear full-unit expansion into habitats, phonics, culture, values, or later textbook lessons was identified in the initial review.

Do **not** confuse incidental animal descriptions with evidence that a later curriculum section was formally taught.
